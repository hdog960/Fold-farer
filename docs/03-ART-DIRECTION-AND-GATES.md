# FoldFarer — Art direction approval and milestone gates

Updated 2026-11-12.

## Accepted artistic direction (creator feedback)
The creator explicitly said: **"I like the art style of the game. Aesthetically I'm pleased."** This authorizes treating the current original layered, hand-painted paper-diorama look as a stable visual target. Prefer scene cohesion, readable acting and high-value repairs; do not perform endless style reinventions.

Retain:
- Original papercraft geometry, visibly constructed fold/ink/watercolor materials, colorful storybook environments and genuine screen-depth exploration.
- Distinct Tavi facing/stride families and cohesive proportions, Morrow's expressive feather-cut silhouette and consistent paper materials, grounded near/far character layering.
- Scenery readability: character must remain visible around archways, trees, shops, bridges and UI. Original style should not cost unusable FPS on iPhone.

## Separate approvals
1. **Artistic direction:** Positive creator feedback received on 2026-11-12.
2. **Overall Milestone A acceptance:** **NOT APPROVED**. A fully playable vertical slice is still incomplete.
3. **Physical iPhone performance and controls:** **NOT VERIFIED AS APPROVED**. User reports that performance improved; full device check remains outstanding.
4. **Milestone B release authorization:** **LOCKED** until the creator explicitly approves.
5. **90/100 illustration rubric:** Target retained from existing planning. Do not manufacture a score, mark passed based on code/screenshots, or use it to override direct creator feedback.

## Review evidence
Collect genuine in-engine landscape screenshots, not mockups. Judge sprite consistency at rest, walking and turns, environments and foreground occlusion, readability, two-thumb comfort, animation motion/reduced motion, and performance. Changes to visual assets must be playable and checked at multiple small iPhone viewport sizes; large dense additions require a performance review.

Stop at the approval gate and ask the creator to inspect the actual live game. Approval applies to the reviewed build; do not infer that it covers unreviewed changes.
